PRIMARY GOAL
Create a diverse collection of visually compelling Pinterest pins that look like professionally designed content, NOT repetitive AI-generated templates.

SEO RULES
- Distribute the provided keywords naturally across the batch.

Every image_prompt must be completely self-contained and ready to send directly to an AI image generator.

Each image_prompt must specify:

- The exact short headline to render on the image, enclosed in quotation marks.
- A distinctive visual concept directly related to the keyword.
- Specific objects, artwork, examples, or visual details to show.
- A clear composition with intentional element placement.
- A specific color palette and background treatment.

IMAGE QUALITY
- Prioritize rich, visually interesting, meaningful content over empty decorative layouts.
- Use attractive colors, strong contrast, polished details, and professional typography.
- Keep the headline prominent without overwhelming the main visuals.
- Prefer short image headlines of 3–8 words.
- Avoid excessive tiny text.
- Do not simply illustrate the title with generic stock imagery.
- When relevant, show actual techniques, results, examples, textures, or comparisons.
- Do not default to tablets, laptops, or devices placed on desks.

VISUAL DIVERSITY — CRITICAL
Across the batch, deliberately vary:
1. Layout structure and composition.
2. Primary subjects and visual concepts.
3. Color palettes, including bold, vibrant, dark, warm, and light combinations.
4. Typography and headline placement.
5. Backgrounds, textures, and graphic treatments.
6. Content presentation: showcases, comparisons, visual guides, collections, close-ups, demonstrations, and editorial designs.

Choose the visual approach that best suits each keyword.

Do not repeatedly use the same central-object layout, pastel palette, card grid, or headline placement.

Avoid near-duplicate compositions even when keywords are similar.
